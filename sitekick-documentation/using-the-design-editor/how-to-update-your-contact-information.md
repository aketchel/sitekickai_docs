# How to Update your Contact Information

1. Open the **Design Editor** from the **Site Editor**.
2. Navigate to the page where your contact info is displayed (typically the homepage or contact page).
3. Click on the text box containing the contact info.
4. Update your **phone number**, **email**, or **address** as needed.
5. Click **Update** to publish your changes.

Additionally, you can update your contact information in the **Contact Forms** via the **Site Editor** left-hand menu to update the default contact email under **Forms > Settings**.
