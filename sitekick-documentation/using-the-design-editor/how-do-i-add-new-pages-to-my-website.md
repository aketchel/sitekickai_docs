# How do I Add New Pages to My Website?

1. From the **Site Editor**, go to **Pages > Add New**.
2. Give your page a name and click **“Edit with Design Editor”**.
3. Design your new page using pre-built sections or drag-and-drop widgets.
4. Click **“Publish”**.

You can also add it to your site menu under **Appearance > Menus**.
