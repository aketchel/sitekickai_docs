# How to Check and Restore Previous Version of Your Website?

To check and restore previous version/s of your website, follow the procedures provided.

To restore a previous version:

1. Open the **Design Editor**.
2. Click on the **History** icon (clock symbol) at the bottom left of the editor panel.
3. Choose a previous version or autosave from the list and click **Restore**.

Note: Only the **Design Editor** content is versioned this way. For full site backups, see “How to Backup Your Site?”
