# Can I setup e-commerce with my Sitekick AI website or landing page?

1. Go to your **Site Editor** then click on the **Ecommerce** tab on the left-hand menu.
2. Follow the setup wizard to add products, payment gateways, and shipping methods.
3. Design your product/shop pages using the **Design Editor**.
4. Customize your cart, checkout, and thank-you pages easily.
