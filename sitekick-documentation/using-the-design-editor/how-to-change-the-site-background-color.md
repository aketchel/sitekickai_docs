# How to Change the Site Background/Color?

Follow the steps below to change your site's background/color.

1. Open the **Design Editor**.
2. Click the gear icon ⚙️ in the bottom left to open **Site Settings**.
3. Go to **Global Colors** or **Background Settings**.
4. Adjust your background globally or per section.
