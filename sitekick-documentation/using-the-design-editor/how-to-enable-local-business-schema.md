# How to Enable Local Business Schema?

1. In the **Site Editor**, go to **SEO > Search Appearance > General**.
2. Under **Organization or Person**, select **Organization**.
3. Fill in business name, logo, and contact details.
4. Our SEO system will auto-generate Local Business Schema for Google.
