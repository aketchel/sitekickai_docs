# Where to Manage Your Website's Privacy Settings?

* Go to the **Site Editor**.
* Navigate to **Settings** > **Privacy**.
* You can edit or generate a Privacy Policy here.
* If needed, you can also manage cookie notices by clicking on the "Privacy" tab on the left hand menu of the Site Editor to meet GDPR/CCPA compliance.
