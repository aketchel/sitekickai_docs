# How to Change your Logo

1. Log in to your **Site Editor**.
2. Navigate to the website you want to edit.
3. Click **Open Design Editor**
4. Locate the logo section on the top left of your **Header** area.
5. Click on the logo to open the image settings.
6. In the left panel, click **Choose Image**.
   * Upload your new logo or choose one from the media library.
   * Click **Insert Media** and then click **Update** to save changes.
