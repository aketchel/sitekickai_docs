# How Can I Change the Logos/Images on the Page?

You can easily swap out logos or images directly in the Design Editor. Here's how:

1. **Log in to your Site Editor**.
2. From the dashboard, click **Design Editor** to launch the visual builder.
3. Click on the logo or image you want to change.
4. In the **left panel**, under “Content,” click **Choose Image**.
5. Either upload a new image or select one from your media library.
6. Adjust sizing, alignment, and other settings as needed.
7. Click **Update** to save your changes.

> ✅ Tip: For the logo, you can also go to `Site Settings` > `Site Identity` to upload your primary and favicon logo across the entire site.
