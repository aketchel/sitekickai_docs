# How do I Generate sites?

Open [Sitekick CMS](https://cms.sitekick.ai/) and choose the website-creation flow available to your account. Name the website, choose the requested address and describe your business, audience and visitor goal. A custom domain remains pending until its separate setup, DNS and ownership checks are complete.

Continue in the website workspace, review the blueprint and generation estimate, then generate a private candidate. Preview each page before approving the exact release and publishing. Follow [Whole-site projects](whole-site-projects.md) for the full sequence and saved-work controls.

For a single landing page or a few individual drafts, use **Create a page** or [Your Sitekick](your-sitekick-conversations.md). Give approved business facts, service names, audience, tone and the action visitors should take. State any text or prices that must stay unchanged. Review the result rather than relying on a fixed generation-time promise.

Planning and generation use credits; website creation also depends on available plan slots and current account access.
