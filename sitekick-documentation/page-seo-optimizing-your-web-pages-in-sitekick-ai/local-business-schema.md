# Local Business Schema

Local Business Schema was created by Search Engines to better understand and display data to the public. Schema is important as this shares data that is important for ranking such as:

* Business Type/Industry
* Name of your business
* Business Address
* Business Contact Information
* Business Logo

Such are only examples of information that is available in the schema.&#x20;

In Sitekick AI you have the option to manage your Local Business Schema in your Site Editor:

Schema markup tells Google what your business does. Sitekick automatically:

* Adds LocalBusiness Schema for your industry
* Includes business name, phone number, address, and hours

You can customize this via:\
**Site Editor → SEO Settings → Local Schema**

This helps you appear in the “Local Pack” on Google Maps.
