# Meta Title and Description

_In case you are wondering what a Meta Title and a Meta Description look like, here is a sample below:_<br>

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (224).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Meta Title:** AI landing page builder

**Meta Description:** _Sitekick_ is superb! The quickest way to get a professional landing page. It offers engaging copy-write, beautiful design and great structure.

Are you now getting the idea? This is why this is a vital part of your SEO and something that you need to optimize. Fortunately, Sitekick AI makes it easy for you to optimize your Meta properties.

Go to **Site Editor → SEO Settings → Meta Tags** to update:

* **Meta Title**: Short, catchy, and keyword-rich (up to 60 characters)
* **Meta Description**: Clear and inviting (up to 155 characters)

Pro tip: Use a question or a call to action like “Book your free estimate today!”
