# Can Sitekick use any languages other than English?

Yes! Sitekick supports automatic multilingual translation through **GTranslate**, which is built into the platform.

**How It Works:**

1. Navigate to your **Site Editor > Settings > GTranslate**.
2. Choose the languages you want to offer on your website.
3. GTranslate uses Google Translate to instantly translate your site into the selected languages.
4. A language switcher will appear on your website, allowing visitors to select their preferred language.
