# How to Change the Editor Language?

You can change the language of your **Site Editor** and **Design Editor** to work in your preferred language. Here's how:

**1. Change Site Editor Language:**

1. Go to your **Site Editor**.
2. Navigate to **Settings > General**.
3. Scroll down to **Site Language**.
4. Select your desired language from the dropdown.
5. Click **Save Changes**.

> This will change the language of the Site Editor interface for your site.

**2. Change Design Editor Language:**

1. Open any page with the **Design Editor**.
2. On the left panel, click the hamburger menu (≡) in the top-left.
3. Go to **Preferences > Language**.
4. Select your preferred language from the list.

> This changes the Design Editor’s UI (labels, menus, etc.) for your account.
