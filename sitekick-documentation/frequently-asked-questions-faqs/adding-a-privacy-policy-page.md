# Adding a Privacy Policy Page

Adding a Privacy Policy page is simple and recommended for legal compliance. To add or update the privacy page on your site:



1. In your Sitekick AI Site Editor dashboard, go to Pages > Add New.
2. Title the page “Privacy Policy.”
3. Paste in your policy content. If you don’t have one, you can use a free generator like Termly or PrivacyPolicies.com.
4.  Once you’ve added the content, click Publish.

    (Optional but recommended) Add this page to your site’s footer:
5.  Go to Appearance > Menus.

    Add your Privacy Policy page to the Footer Menu.

    Click Save Menu.

Now your visitors can access your privacy policy from the bottom of your site
