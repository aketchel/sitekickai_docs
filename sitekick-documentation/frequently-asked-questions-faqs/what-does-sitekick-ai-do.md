# What does Sitekick AI do?

Sitekick CMS helps you create and improve a WordPress website with AI. Use **Your Sitekick** to plan work in a saved conversation, prepare drafts, compare alternatives and approve changes. You can work on individual pages or a coordinated whole-site project, and open advanced visual or WordPress tools when needed.

Generation, account permissions and enabled features determine which actions are available. Review business facts, design and links before applying or publishing a result; generation time varies with the work and queue.

Start with [Work with Your Sitekick](../working-with-our-ai-site-editor/your-sitekick-conversations.md). For external assistants, read [MCP editing setup](../working-with-our-ai-site-editor/mcp-editing.md). Studio custom applications remain unlaunched; ongoing CMS AI editing is already available in CMS.
