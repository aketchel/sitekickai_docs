# How many domains/websites can I host with Sitekick?

You can host as many websites and domains as you need with Sitekick AI. Our Lite plan provides hosting for 1 website, our Basic plan provides hosting for 3 websites, and our Pro plan provides hosting for up to 10 websites. If you need hosting for more than 10, you can get multiple subscriptions or contact us at Support@Sitekick.ai to create a custom solution for you.&#x20;
