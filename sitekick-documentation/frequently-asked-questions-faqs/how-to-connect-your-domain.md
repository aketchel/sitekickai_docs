# How to Connect your Domain?

1. On the "My Websites" page, navigate to the site you wish to connect your custom domain to then click **"Add Domain"**&#x20;
2. Type in the domain you'd like to link to your site, then click **"Save Domain"**.&#x20;
3. Once you've connected the domain, click **"Domain Config"** next to **"Site Editor"** and copy and paste the A Name Record to your domain's DNS records on your Domain provider, whether Godaddy, Namecheap etc.
4. After connecting the A Record, be sure to delete any old records connected to the custom domain to ensure they don't interfere with your new domain connection with your Sitekick AI site. Within 15-30 minutes, your DNS records should be fully record and reflect on your domain.&#x20;
