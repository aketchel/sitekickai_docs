# Reviewing Your Site

Reviewing is where your site becomes yours. Studio drafts fast; your taste
and knowledge of the business are what make the result right.

This section covers the two halves of reviewing:

- [Reviewing the AI draft](reviewing-the-ai-draft.md) — what to look at on
  each pass.
- [Requesting changes](requesting-changes.md) — how to give feedback that
  produces the changes you want.

After the site looks right, continue to [Publishing](../publishing/README.md).
