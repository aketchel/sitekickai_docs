# Choosing between Sitekick AI CMS and Studio

**Sitekick CMS is deployed. Studio custom applications remain unlaunched and inactive.** Studio marketing or a waitlist does not provide an active Studio account.

CMS supports ongoing AI editing through **Your Sitekick**, saved conversations, reviewable tasks, page alternatives and whole-site projects. You can combine those workflows with advanced visual and WordPress tools; conversational editing is not reserved for Studio.

Use [Work with Your Sitekick](https://docs.sitekick.ai/working-with-our-ai-site-editor/your-sitekick-conversations) for current CMS help. [CMS MCP editing](https://docs.sitekick.ai/working-with-our-ai-site-editor/mcp-editing) requires separate service setup, supported client registration and authorization; do not assume it is immediately available to every account.

Studio is the future custom-application direction. The other Studio pages describe proposed workflows, not verified live features, plan entitlements or a reason to move a working CMS website today. Wait for an explicit availability announcement before relying on those workflows.
