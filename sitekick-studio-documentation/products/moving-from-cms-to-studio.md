# Moving from Sitekick AI CMS to Studio

Studio custom applications remain unlaunched and inactive. There is no currently documented customer migration workflow to perform. Do not change your domain, retire a CMS site or assume account, plan or content transfer based on the proposed Studio guides.

Continue using [Sitekick CMS](https://docs.sitekick.ai/): ongoing conversational editing and whole-site projects are CMS features. See [Work with Your Sitekick](https://docs.sitekick.ai/working-with-our-ai-site-editor/your-sitekick-conversations).

If Studio becomes available, review its announced eligibility, migration support and domain procedure before making a change. A waitlist records interest; it is not an active paid Studio account.
