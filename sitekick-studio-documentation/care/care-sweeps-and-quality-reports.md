# Care sweeps and quality reports

Care sweeps are Studio's way of looking after your site after launch. A
sweep reviews the live site; a quality report summarizes what it found.

## What a sweep reviews

Each sweep looks at the site the way a careful professional would:

- **Pages and links** — that pages load and nothing links to a dead end.
- **Content freshness** — copy that has gone stale, or details that may have
  changed since launch.
- **Search readiness** — how the site describes itself to search engines,
  and whether the basics are in place.

## What a quality report tells you

A quality report turns the sweep's findings into a short, readable summary:
what is healthy, what needs attention, and what Studio recommends. The
report is written for you — not for a web developer — so you can act on it
directly.

## What to do with findings

Nothing in a report is urgent by default. A reasonable rhythm:

1. Read the report when it arrives.
2. Accept the suggestions that make sense to you.
3. Ignore or dismiss the ones that do not fit your business.

Over time this loop — sweep, report, approve — is what keeps a Studio site
from slowly going stale, the way most small-business websites do.
