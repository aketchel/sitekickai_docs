# Site Care

Publishing is not the end of the work — it is the start of the site's life.
Sitekick AI Studio keeps working on a published site for you.

This section covers:

- [Care sweeps and quality reports](care-sweeps-and-quality-reports.md) —
  how Studio reviews your live site over time.

## What care looks like

After launch, Studio periodically reviews the site the way a good agency
would: checking that pages are healthy, that the copy still fits, and that
nothing has quietly broken. When it finds something, it tells you — and
often proposes a fix.

## You stay in charge

Care never publishes surprises to your site. Improvements are proposed, and
you decide what to accept. Studio does the work of noticing; you keep the
judgment of approving.

See [Care sweeps and quality reports](care-sweeps-and-quality-reports.md)
for the details of what gets reviewed and reported.
