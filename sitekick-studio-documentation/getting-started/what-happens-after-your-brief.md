# What happens after you submit a brief

When you submit your brief, Sitekick AI Studio gets to work on a first draft
of your site.

## What Studio drafts

From your brief, Studio builds:

- **The page structure** — which pages the site needs and how they are
  organized.
- **The copy** — headlines, descriptions, and page text written for your
  business and audience.
- **The design** — a layout and visual treatment that fits the tone of the
  brief.

Drafts are starting points, not verdicts. Every part of the draft can be
revised — see [Requesting changes](../review/requesting-changes.md).

## The review loop

Studio's workflow is a loop, not a one-shot build:

1. Studio prepares a draft.
2. You review it and say what to change.
3. Studio revises and you look again.
4. When it feels right, you publish.

You can stay in the loop as long as you want. Some people approve a draft
after one pass; others refine for days. Both are normal.

## While the draft is being prepared

Drafting takes a little time — you do not need to keep the page open. When
the draft is ready, it appears in your workspace for review.

If a draft misses the mark entirely, it usually means the brief was too
thin. Add detail about what the business does and who it serves, then ask
for a revision.
